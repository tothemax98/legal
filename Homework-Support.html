Need help with scanning, explanations, or a subscription? Email maximumware@gmail.com.

Scanning homework

Use a clear, well-lit photo with the entire question visible. You can take a photo or choose an existing image. An internet connection is required for AI help. Check AI-generated answers against your course materials.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen to restore an active subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account's subscription settings. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

When contacting support

Include your app version, device model, and a description of the problem. If you attach a screenshot, remove personal information. Never send passwords or full payment information.
